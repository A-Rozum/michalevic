Get in touch.

A short message or call to our manager is enough to begin. Describe your situation and ask the questions on your mind.

How we can talk

Start with your situation.

Our manager will listen and help you understand whether the office can take on your case and what kind of assistance it can offer. If you need legal advice, the manager can arrange a consultation with Ales Michalevic or a specialist lawyer, depending on your questions.

Much of the office’s work involves preparing legal documents, so its lawyers work from different cities and countries. If working remotely does not suit you, agree on a convenient time and place for an in-person meeting with the manager before travelling to the office.

Before visiting

A few practical details.

Do I need to book ahead?

Yes. The manager will help arrange a convenient time and confirm the meeting place before you travel.

Can we meet outside Vilnius?

An in-person meeting elsewhere depends on your location and the lawyers’ availability. Ask the manager about the options; you can also start with an online consultation.

Which documents should I send?

Electronic copies, such as scans or photos, are what the team needs. The manager will provide a detailed list for your case. If you have many documents, they can help prioritise what to send first and suggest how best to share the copies.
