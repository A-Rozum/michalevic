Experience behind the practice

Expertise shaped by experience.

Ales Michalevic founded the office after facing international pursuit himself. That experience underpins the office’s particular attention to people contesting politically motivated or unfounded requests.

The office works with many lawyers and human-rights specialists in different countries on international-search and extradition matters. It connects the facts, documents and proceedings across jurisdictions to build a strategy for each case.

First questions

Your questions, answered.

Does the office only work on INTERPOL matters?

The office handles a range of cross-border matters, including INTERPOL notices, extradition, asylum and human-rights protection. Related legal issues can be considered as part of the same case.

Where can we meet in person?

Contact the office to agree on a suitable time and place for an in-person meeting before travelling.

What affects the outcome of a case?

In cross-border matters, several factors shape the outcome at once: the countries involved, the stage of proceedings and the available evidence. The first step is to assess those circumstances and choose an approach suited to the case.
